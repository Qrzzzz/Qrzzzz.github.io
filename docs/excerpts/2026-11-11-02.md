I wasn’t prepared to say much at first, whereas many other students were much less hesitant and seemed to know what they were talking about. A couple of weeks later, after having gotten through a big chunk of the textbook, which was written by Spanier himself, I realized that most of the students were just spouting nonsense—showing off, in other words.

Shing-Tung Yau and Steve Nadis, *The Shape of a Life: One Mathematician’s Search for the Universe’s Hidden Geometry* (Yale University Press, 2019), Chapter 3, “Coming to America,” p. 49.
