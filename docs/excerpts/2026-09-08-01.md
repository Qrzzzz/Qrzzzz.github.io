<blockquote class="excerpt-quotation" lang="en">

The reasonable man adapts himself to the world; the unreasonable one persists in trying to adapt the world to himself.

Therefore, all progress depends on the unreasonable man.

<footer>George Bernard Shaw, <cite><a href="https://books.google.com/books/about/Man_and_Superman.html?id=4Y2BPKolHEkC">Man and Superman</a></cite>, “Maxims for Revolutionists,” “Reason,” p. 234, 1903</footer>
