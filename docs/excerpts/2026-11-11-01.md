<article class="excerpt-entry excerpt-entry--markdown" id="excerpt-2026-11-11-01" lang="en" aria-label="Excerpt">

You never really understand a person until you consider things from his point of view—until you climb into his skin and walk around in it.

<div class="excerpt-attribution">

Harper Lee, *To Kill a Mockingbird* (1960), Chapter 3.

</div>

<section class="excerpt-translations" aria-label="中文译文">

<section class="excerpt-translation" lang="zh-CN" aria-label="中文译文">

你永远无法真正理解一个人，除非你从他的角度考虑问题——除非你钻进他的皮肤里，像他一样走来走去。

</section>
